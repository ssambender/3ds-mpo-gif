Instructions:
1
Choose your source images. You can use one 3DS .MPO file, two matching JPG/PNG images, or three matching JPG/PNG images from a Nimslo-style camera.
Keep the camera as level as possible for the cleanest wiggle.
2
For 3DS photos, upload a single .MPO file. If needed, copy it from the SD card or transfer it by FTP first.
3DS photos can usually be found in the DCIM folder.
3
For two-image uploads, select the left image first and the right image second. The app can add an optional blended interpolation frame between them.
4
For three-image uploads, select the images in order: left, middle, right. The app uses the real middle image as the middle frame.
5
After uploading, drag the adjustment sliders until the focal point is as dark as possible. Then choose frame length and create your GIF.
6
View and download your wigglegram as a GIF. You can also go back to readjust alignment before downloading.
Wigglegram Generator (3DS Compatible)
Upload an .mpo image taken from a 3DS, or select two/three matching .jpg/.png images, and magically create a full-resolution Wigglegram GIF!

*All data is processed locally and is not sent/saved online.
